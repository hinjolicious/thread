Glory led men, brightest of fires;
Desire dwelt within the breast—war was not its only hunger.
The battle-bold king was wounded within;
Fate and will wrestled together.
Gold could not satisfy him, nor boasting make him whole;
A woman's love was sweet, yet sorrow followed after.
The dragon arose, as deep sins arise;
Bone-house broke, bright glory fell.
Yet fame, like stone, lives after death;
A man's own heart is his greatest enemy.
